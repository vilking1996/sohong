Article 1: Objectives of the Sustainable Development and UFO Savings Fund

1.1. The primary objective of the Fund is to mobilize and manage a total capital of USD 1 billion from individual and institutional investors to invest in sustainable development projects, including renewable energy, environmental protection, sustainable communities, and advanced technology research initiatives such as UFO technology and space exploration.

1.2. Key investment sectors include:

  • Renewable Energy: Investments in solar power, wind energy, small-scale hydropower, and other clean energy solutions.
  • UFO Technology and Space Exploration: Research and development of UFO-related technologies, space vehicles, and interplanetary exploration projects.
  • Community Infrastructure and Quality of Life: Initiatives in education, healthcare, and sustainable infrastructure development.
Article 2: Investment Structure

2.1. The Fund shall allocate the mobilized capital in the following proportions:

  • 40% to renewable energy and green technology projects (USD 400 million).
  • 30% to UFO and space technology projects (USD 300 million).
  • 20% to sustainable community development and infrastructure projects (USD 200 million).
  • 10% for reserves and operational expenses (USD 100 million).

4.1. Investor Rights: Investors are entitled to receive periodic financial reports, investment performance updates, and information on funded projects. Investors may participate in strategic investment decisions as defined by the Fund’s governance framework.

4.2. Investor Obligations: Investors commit to complying with all contractual terms, withdrawal conditions, and the sustainable development objectives of the Fund.

Article 5: Interest Rate and Profit Distribution

5.1. The minimum annual interest rate shall be 5%, subject to adjustment based on the financial performance of the Fund’s investments.

Article 6: Cooperation with the World Bank

6.1. The Fund shall cooperate with the World Bank in the implementation and oversight of global sustainable development and space technology initiatives, ensuring transparency, accountability, and efficient capital utilization.

Article 7: Legal Provisions

7.1. The Fund shall operate in accordance with applicable international financial, banking, and environmental regulations.

7.2. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through mutual negotiation. Failing such resolution, disputes shall be submitted to a competent court of jurisdiction.
